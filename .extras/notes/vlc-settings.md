### VLC settings

- **Preferences → Interface:** Disable **Resize interface to video size** and enable **Use dark palette**.
- **Subtitles / OSD:** Disable **Show media title on video start**.
- **Hotkeys:** Set **Next** → `Right Arrow` and **Previous** → `Left Arrow`.
  - Rebind **Navigate Left/Right** to `Shift+Left/Right`.
  - This replaces **Very short forward/backward jump**, which is largely redundant since VLC already has short, medium, and long jumps.
